# Brand Voice

Use this reference when rewriting or expanding website copy for peterschmalfeldt.com.

## Site purpose

The site has two equally important jobs:

1. Help people find Peter when searching for a senior full-stack engineer, AI developer, solutions architect, or technical consultant.
2. Help visitors understand Peter's value, experience, and working style once they arrive.

The site is not just a portfolio. It is a credibility engine and a conversion tool.

## Core positioning

Peter should come across as:

- A senior full-stack engineer with deep real-world experience.
- An AI-focused builder who can design and ship practical solutions, not just talk about AI strategy.
- A solutions architect who can connect technical decisions to business needs.
- A consultant who works well with teams, improves clarity, and helps organizations move from ambiguity to execution.

## Audience

Primary audiences:

- Founders and business owners who need a senior technical partner.
- Engineering leaders who need a hands-on architect or high-leverage senior engineer.
- Teams looking for AI implementation help, workflow automation, or product strategy grounded in delivery.
- Companies with ecommerce, platform, integration, or modernization needs.

## Voice characteristics

Write in a voice that is:

- First-person
- Experienced
- Practical
- Calm
- Clear
- Confident without being flashy
- Human and collaborative

The voice should suggest: "I have done this before, I can help, and I know how to work with people."

## What the writing should emphasize

- Hands-on technical ability across front-end, back-end, architecture, and integrations.
- AI specialization that is implementation-oriented and business-relevant.
- The ability to lead discovery, align teams, and move projects forward.
- A history of building useful systems, not just shipping code for its own sake.
- What it feels like to work with Peter: practical, thoughtful, collaborative, and outcome-focused.

## Style patterns already present on the site

The existing site often does these things well:

- Connects technical work to real business or organizational outcomes.
- Balances technical language with plain-English explanation.
- Describes both what Peter builds and how he works with teams.
- Uses confident but measured claims instead of hype-heavy language.
- Moves between delivery, leadership, consulting, and strategy without losing the sense that Peter is still a builder.

## Tone calibration

Lean toward:

- "I build", "I design", "I guide", "I help", "I specialize"
- Concrete outcomes like clarity, efficiency, scalability, alignment, performance, reliability, and better customer experience
- Service language that feels personal and direct

Avoid:

- "rockstar", "ninja", "guru", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary"
- Empty AI phrasing like "unlock the power of AI" unless paired with a concrete outcome
- Overly polished agency phrasing such as "we deliver transformative digital experiences"
- Keyword stuffing
- Long walls of text when a tighter paragraph would say it better

## SEO guidance

Important concepts to reflect naturally when relevant:

- Senior Full-Stack Engineer
- Full-Stack Web Developer
- AI Developer
- AI Engineer
- Solutions Architect
- Ecommerce Development
- Shopify
- Salesforce Commerce Cloud
- Technical Consulting
- Prompt Engineering
- Automation
- Web Application Development

Use the most relevant terms for the section. Do not force all of them into one page.

## Conversion guidance

A strong page or section should answer:

- What can Peter help with?
- Why should someone trust him?
- What kind of problems does he solve?
- What is it like to work with him?
- What should the visitor do next?

CTAs should feel direct and helpful, not pushy.

## Evidence from the current site

These recurring themes already fit the target voice:

- Peter links technical execution to real business needs.
- Peter combines hands-on development with leadership and consulting.
- Peter emphasizes practical outcomes, cross-functional alignment, and collaboration.
- Peter is positioned as someone who can build, guide, and unblock teams.
- Testimonials reinforce teaching, clarity, productivity, trust, breadth, and quality.

## Editing checklist

Before finalizing copy, check:

- Does this help both search discovery and human understanding?
- Does it sound like one experienced person speaking directly?
- Does it keep AI prominent without becoming generic AI marketing copy?
- Does it say what Peter does, who he helps, and how he works?
- Does it stay specific and believable?
- Does the CTA still fit the surrounding content?
