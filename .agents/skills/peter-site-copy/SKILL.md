# Peter Site Copy

Use this skill for any copy change that affects how the site is found, understood, or trusted.

## Required context

Before rewriting copy, read `references/brand-voice.md`.

When making page-level changes, also inspect the surrounding page so the new copy matches the section flow, headings, and CTA style.

## Core objective

Every copy update should support both goals equally:

1. Help the right people find Peter through search.
2. Help visitors quickly understand what Peter can do and what it is like to work with him.

Do not optimize for SEO at the expense of clarity. Do not optimize for cleverness at the expense of discoverability.

## Positioning rules

- Write in first person singular unless the user explicitly asks for a third-person bio.
- Present Peter as a senior hands-on builder, not just a strategist.
- Keep AI expertise prominent, but tie it to practical implementation and business outcomes.
- Preserve the mix of technical depth, leadership experience, and consulting pragmatism.
- Favor specific capabilities and outcomes over broad claims.

## Tone rules

- Sound experienced, direct, practical, and credible.
- Keep the voice warm and human, but not casual or chatty.
- Avoid inflated agency language, startup cliches, and generic AI hype.
- Avoid sounding like a resume dump or a list of buzzwords.
- Keep confidence grounded in real experience, outcomes, and collaboration style.

## Workflow

1. Identify the search intent behind the page or section.
2. Identify the visitor question the copy needs to answer.
3. Keep or strengthen important keyword phrases naturally in titles, headings, intro copy, and metadata.
4. Rewrite for clarity, credibility, and conversion.
5. Check whether nearby CTAs, title tags, descriptions, or headings should change too.

## SEO guidance

- Keep primary terms natural and readable.
- Prefer phrases like `Senior Full-Stack Engineer`, `AI Developer`, `AI Engineer`, `Solutions Architect`, `Ecommerce Development`, and `Consulting` when they match the section.
- Strengthen semantic relevance with adjacent supporting terms instead of repeating the same keyword mechanically.
- If a page change materially shifts positioning, update frontmatter `title`, `description`, and `keywords` too.

## Editing guardrails

- Do not invent clients, outcomes, certifications, or years of experience.
- Do not remove the human, collaborative aspects of the copy.
- Do not turn the voice into corporate "we" language.
- Do not make Peter sound like an agency, recruiter, or generic freelancer marketplace profile.

## Output expectations

When making meaningful copy changes:

- Keep headings concise.
- Keep paragraphs tight and scannable.
- Preserve clear CTA language.
- Briefly explain the copy strategy in the final response.
